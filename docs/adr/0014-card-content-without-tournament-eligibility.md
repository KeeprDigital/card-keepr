# Catalogue card content without tournament eligibility

Accepted through the [owner-approved scope amendment in Decide bounded durable reconciliation and publication](https://github.com/KeeprDigital/card-keepr/issues/214#issuecomment-5558719187), which holds the complete decision. The owner consumes card facts, not tournament eligibility: exclude ban lists, organised-play restrictions, legality computation, FAQs and rulings, while retaining original printed text and publisher-corrected content through Errata.

This supersedes legality obligations in ADRs 0001 and 0003 and earlier authority, admission and consumer-visibility plans; unrelated evidence, identity, history and recovery protections remain. The pre-Go-Live specification must remove the obsolete acquisition dependencies, contracts, processing and validation together; this is a planning decision, and the current implementation still contains that machinery.
